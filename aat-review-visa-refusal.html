AAT Review

Professional support for AAT Review

Visa refusal and review support, including refusal letter review, merits review preparation and evidence strategy. Austrix helps clients understand the pathway, prepare relevant evidence and avoid common mistakes that can delay or weaken an application.

Visa requirements can change, and every matter depends on personal circumstances, evidence and current Department of Home Affairs criteria. Our team helps you understand your options with care and clarity.

Best next step

Share your current visa status, education or work background, and main migration goal. The team can then recommend a focused consultation direction.

Documents usually reviewed

Identity

Passport, birth certificate, national ID, relationship documents and previous visa records.

Evidence

Study, employment, financial, English, skills, sponsorship or relationship evidence depending on the visa type.

Compliance

Health insurance, police checks, medicals, declarations and any requested additional information.
