About Austrix

Austrix Migration & Education Consulting supports clients who want clear direction across Australian study, visa and migration pathways. The goal is to make complex processes easier to understand while helping clients prepare stronger evidence before they proceed.

Austrix provides clear, professional and client-focused support for students, skilled professionals, families, employers and visitors.

Our approach

  • Clear pathway explanation.
  • Practical document preparation.
  • Ethical and evidence-based guidance.
  • Responsive support for clients and families.
